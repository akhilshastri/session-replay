How to use
  • Open any page straight from disk. No server, no network.
  • Theme menu (top right): System follows your OS live. Light and Dark are saved and shared by all pages.
  • The dashed MOCK pill at the bottom left switches variants on pages that have them. A link such as 03-replay-playing.html#hover opens one directly.
  • Tabs, the Layout and zoom menus, chips and the speed control work. The replay itself does not play.
